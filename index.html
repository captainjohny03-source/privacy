Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers the app "GRAY CODA TV" (the "App"). The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Page "Grey Coda Tv" to schedule and publish the owner's own content on that Page.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, store, or share personal information from visitors, followers, or any other Facebook users. It does not read comments, messages, or profile data of other people.

How the App is used

The App connects to the Facebook Graph API with the Page owner's permission, solely to publish images and captions that the Page owner has prepared. Access credentials are stored in a private, access-restricted location and are not shared with anyone.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information with third parties.

Data deletion

Because the App does not collect personal data from users, there is no user data to delete. The Page owner can remove the App's access at any time from Facebook Settings, under Business Integrations.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the owner through the Facebook Page "Grey Coda Tv".
